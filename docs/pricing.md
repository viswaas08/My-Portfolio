## 2. Recurring Maintenance Plans

Maintenance begins after project handover and is billed monthly in advance.

### Plan A: Website Care — ₹1,499/month
- **Target:** Clients needing reliable uptime and occasional menu/price tweaks.
- **Inclusions:**
  - Fast cloud hosting configuration and CDN routing
  - Monthly uptime & HTTP health monitoring
  - Minor content updates (up to 3 requests/month: text, pricing, photos)
  - Seasonal banner / holiday hours adjustments
  - Bug fixes and browser compatibility checks
  - Email & WhatsApp technical support (48h SLA)

### Plan B: Advanced Website Care — ₹2,999/month
- **Target:** High-intent local businesses actively competing in local search.
- **Inclusions:**
  - Everything in Website Care
  - Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization and monthly post updates
  - Google Search Console performance monitoring & indexing checks
  - Monthly website speed audit (Core Web Vitals)
  - Bi-weekly content/menu updates (up to 8 requests/month)
  - Priority emergency response (same-day SLA)

## 3. Clear Disclaimers & Ethics Policy

1. **No Guaranteed Google Rankings:** We implement best-practice technical SEO and local structured data. However, Google algorithm updates and search rankings are never guaranteed.
2. **No Guaranteed Revenue/Customers:** A website is a modern digital storefront and trust builder; sales depend on the client's product, pricing, service quality, and local reputation.
3. **Domain Renewal Responsibility:** Domain renewal fees (typically ₹800 - ₹1,200/year) are paid directly by the client to their registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains).
4. **Third-Party Costs:** SMS gateways, paid booking engines, and commercial email suites (Google Workspace) are billed directly by third-party vendors.
