# Client Onboarding System: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

This document explains the 7-step onboarding process designed to convert leads into paying clients smoothly, enforce legal clarity regarding asset ownership, and guarantee advance payment before development begins.

---

## The 7-Step Onboarding Workflow

```
[ Step 1: Client Info ]
        ↓
[ Step 2: Business Info ]
        ↓
[ Step 3: Package & Care Plan ]
        ↓
[ Step 4: Content Checklist ]
        ↓
[ Step 5: Domain Ownership Agreement ]
        ↓
[ Step 6: 50% Advance Payment Terms ]
        ↓
[ Step 7: Registry Record & Handoff ]
```

---

### Step 1: Client Information
Collect primary point-of-contact details:
- Full Name
- Designation (Owner, Partner, General Manager)
- Phone & WhatsApp Number (verified for direct updates)
- Official Email address

### Step 2: Business Profile
Capture local business identity:
- Registered Business Name
- Business Category (Restaurant, Cafe, Bakery, Retail Shop, Salon, Gym, Tuition Centre, Clinic)
- Physical Address & Google Maps Pin
- Current website or domain (if any)

### Step 3: Package & Care Plan Selection
Select appropriate project tier:
- **Starter Website (₹4,999)**: 1–3 pages for small local services.
- **Business Website (₹9,999 - Most Popular)**: 5–7 pages, menu/gallery, WhatsApp integration, local SEO setup.
- **Pro Web Application (₹19,999+)**: React frontend, custom database, auth, admin CRUD.
- **Monthly Maintenance**:
  - Website Care (₹1,499/mo)
  - Advanced Website Care (₹2,999/mo)

### Step 4: Digital Content Checklist
Ensure client understands required inputs:
1. High-resolution Business Logo (.PNG, SVG, or vector)
2. Professional interior & exterior photos (or high-quality mobile shots)
3. Complete Product Menu / Services with prices
4. Official opening hours and holiday schedules
5. Social media account handles (Instagram, Facebook)
6. Access to Google Business Profile (or authorization to set it up)

## Asset Ownership Charter

To avoid misunderstandings, the platform enforces strict contractual division:

### Client-Owned Assets (100% Client Property)
- **Domain Name**: Registered in the client's name and payment method.
- **Google Business Profile & Google Search Console**: Owned by client's email account.
- **Business Email & Social Accounts**: Instagram, WhatsApp Business, Facebook.
- **Raw Business Content & Photos**: Menus, prices, trademarks, brand collateral.

### Freelancer-Managed Technical Assets
- **Application Source Code**: Maintained in GitHub repository.
- **Deployment & CDN**: Hosted on commercial Vercel/Netlify team infrastructure.
- **Technical Maintenance**: DNS records, SSL certificates, build pipelines, caching policies.

> **Crucial Rule:** We never purchase client domains under personal registrar accounts. Clients purchase and own their domain; we provide DNS records (A & CNAME) for pointing.

## Payment Terms & 50/50 Handover Model

All freelance projects operate on the industry-standard 50% milestone model:

1. **50% Advance Payment**: Required before git repository initialization and design development.
2. **Development & Preview**: Client receives a private staging/preview link (`https://preview.vercel.app`) to review progress.
3. **50% Final Settlement**: Due upon final approval and before pointing the live production domain to the server.
4. **Handover Deliverables**: Production website live, Google Search Console sitemap submitted, Google Business Profile updated.
